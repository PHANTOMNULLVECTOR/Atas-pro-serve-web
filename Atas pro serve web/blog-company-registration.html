Company Registration in Kenya: A Simple Guide

Registering a company in Kenya is the first step towards building a legitimate and trusted business.

Steps to Register a Company in Kenya

  1. Name search and reservation
  2. Prepare company documents
  3. Submit registration via eCitizen
  4. Receive certificate of incorporation
  5. Register for KRA and other statutory bodies

The process can take between 3–7 working days depending on accuracy and approvals.

Why Proper Registration Matters

A registered company builds credibility, allows access to tenders, banking services, and legal protection.
